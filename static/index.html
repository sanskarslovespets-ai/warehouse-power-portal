Pendency is a snapshot of shipments with no terminal status yet, taken at the refresh time above — it ages by the hour, unlike the breach views. Breached = promise timestamp already passed. Source CX_data.CDC_Nugget, one row per ticket, joined to cp_database_N on AWB for the courier column. Scoped to 17 issue / sub-issue pairs covering last-mile faults only: cancellation, delay, failed attempts, MDND, damage and expiry. Missing items and wrong item sent are deliberately excluded as packing/content faults rather than delivery faults. Everything outside those pairs is excluded, so this is not total complaint volume. Courier is attributed in two hops: awb_number matched directly against ClickPost, and where that fails and the value is a DN number, resolved through tabDelivery Note.custom_awb_number back into ClickPost. About 81% of tickets in this scope get a courier; the rest show as Unmapped, mostly because the AWB field contains free text or multiple references. order_id is not used - it is not unique. (blank) is a real value, not an error: category, issue and sub-issue are empty strings on roughly 61-67% of tickets in the source table. Ticket volume jumped about fourfold in Jul 2026; that discontinuity is unexplained and may be an ingestion change rather than a real surge, so trend lines across it are unsafe. Fake attempt rate divides Fake Attempt (Nobody Came) tickets by that courier's delivered shipments over the same dates, so couriers of different size are comparable. It is a floor, not an exact rate: tickets whose courier could not be attributed are excluded from the numerator but the denominator is complete, so every courier's true rate is somewhat higher. Tickets are counted by creation date and deliveries by delivery date, which will not line up exactly for any single day. All timestamps IST.

Event basis. Every shipment is counted on the date it reached a terminal outcome — the rto_mark_date for those that came back, the delivery_date for those that did not — so RTO % = RTOs marked in the period ÷ (RTOs marked + delivered in the period). Shipments still in flight, cancelled, lost or damaged sit on neither side of that ratio and are excluded. Unlike the cohort basis this replaced, recent days are not understated: both sides are settled outcomes. Avg days to mark is rto_mark_date − created_at, a lag measure only.
Returns received at warehouse is the one section on a different clock. It counts RTO-Delivered shipments on rto_delivery_date — the day the parcel physically arrived back — because that is what a warehouse reconciles against. The gap is real: the median mark-to-receipt lag is about four days and only ~21% arrive within a day, so these daily counts will not line up with the mark-date views above, and are not meant to. The cumulative column answers "how much had landed by D−1".
Window is the last 100 days. Delhivery Reverse is excluded throughout. Destinations outside the top 80 by volume are bucketed as Other. Value columns show ClickPost invoice_value (tax inclusive) and the ERP net_total (pre-tax); net is blank where the AWB has no matching delivery note.

Connected means origin_hub_out_timestamp — the shipment leaving the origin hub on a line-haul connection. Note the column is origin hub out: cp_database_N has no "origin city out" field, and this is its only origin-side departure timestamp. Fill is 99% on Bluedart and 94% on Delhivery, and it never precedes pickup_date on any row.
The SLA is an absolute morning cutoff, not an elapsed-time budget. A shipment picked on day D should be connected by 08:00 on D+1. So Day 1 is met, Day 2 is one day late (out by 08:00 on D+2), Day 3+ is later than that, and Not connected has no timestamp at all. That means Day 1 is a ~32-hour window for a midnight pickup and an 8-hour one for a 23:59 pickup — deliberately, because that is how the commitment is stated.
Bluedart and Delhivery only. The SDD/NDD network couriers deliver out of the same city and run no line-haul connection, so the measure does not apply to them.
Counts are per bucket; the percentages are cumulative. Day 1 % is Day 1 alone, By day 2 % is Day 1 + Day 2, and By day 3+ % is everything that ever got a connection scan — so that last figure falls short of 100% by exactly the Not connected share. All three divide by total shipments including the unscanned ones: dividing by scanned-only would flatter precisely the warehouses with the worst scan coverage.
The newest pickup dates are immature and marked provisional: a shipment picked today cannot yet have missed an 08:00-tomorrow deadline, so those rows always look worse than they will settle at. Window is the last 60 days of pickups.

Signing in and finding your way

The portal brings the warehouse's RTO, breach and warehouse dashboards together in one place. You only see the tabs you've been given.

Signing in

  • Use the username and password your admin gave you. The username isn't case-sensitive; the password is (a "Caps Lock is on" warning appears if it's on).
  • A wrong username and a wrong password give the same message, on purpose.
  • After 5 wrong tries in 15 minutes, that username is locked for 15 minutes: Too many wrong attempts. Please wait 15 minutes and try again.

Staying signed in

  • A sign-in lasts 12 hours and renews itself while you use the portal. If you stop using it, you're signed out 6 to 12 hours later.
  • You're also signed out by Sign out, or within about a minute if an admin changes your password or removes your account. You'll see You were signed out. Please sign in again to carry on.

The sidebar

Tabs are grouped into RTO, Breach Explorer and Warehouse Dashboards. Click a group's name to fold it away. The ☰ button at the top left hides the whole sidebar. Admins also see Users. Help is there for everyone.

Common questions

I forgot my password.

There's no self-service reset. Ask your warehouse admin: they set a new password on the Users page and pass it on to you.

I can't see a tab that a colleague can.

It hasn't been ticked for you. Ask an admin to give you that tab on the Users page, then reload the page. If you have no tabs at all, you'll see No tabs yet.

Viewers, editors and admins

Each person has one role, plus the tabs ticked for them.

Viewer
Can look at their tabs and download from them (Export / Download CSV), but can't change anything. Buttons that change data or refresh it are hidden, not greyed out.
Editor
Everything a viewer can do, plus changing data in their tabs (adding entries, uploading invoices, removing an invoice) and the Refresh from DB and Refresh data buttons.
Admin
Every tab, plus Users & access, where people and their access are managed.
What does "Your access to the … tab is view-only" mean?

You tried to change something in a tab where you're a viewer. Ask an admin to make you an editor if you need to.

Why is a button greyed out?

Usually because a refresh is already running (the button says Refreshing…). It comes back when the refresh finishes.

How fresh the data is

Most dashboards are a copy of the systems' data, refreshed on a schedule, so pages open fast. The top bar says how fresh yours is.

The schedule

  • Everything refreshes every day at 09:00 IST; the new data is usually in by about 9:10. If the server was asleep at 9:00, the first visit afterwards catches up.
  • Control Tower also rebuilds every 30 minutes, and BoxVault reloads every hour while it's open.
  • An open page checks for new data every 5 minutes and updates itself, so you don't need to reload.

Why does the top bar show an earlier time than the tab I'm on?

The top bar shows the oldest of all your tabs' data. A tab that refreshes more often (like Control Tower) can be newer.

"This data was refreshed a few minutes ago" — why won't it refresh?

Someone refreshed it within the last 10 minutes. Refreshes are spaced out so the source systems aren't overloaded; try again a little later.

Common questions

"No entries match these filters" — but there is data.

The range is probably too narrow: most sub-tabs open on Last 30 days. Widen the range or clear a filter.

Why don't the numbers match the panel itself?

The panels show all dates by default, while Analytics opens on the last 30 days and can count by a different date (the By: dropdown).

Why does MDND count AWBs as "Checked" that nobody checked?

AWBs logged in the Process form are filled in automatically as "Received and Damaged", which counts as checked.

A red banner says the last database refresh reported a problem.

The latest refresh failed, so you're seeing the last good data (usually the previous day's). It will try again at the next refresh.

"…being fetched from the database for the first time."

That only happens on the very first load after the server starts. The page updates by itself.

Common questions

ERP Status is blank.

The ERP says Return Completed but the courier says in transit.

The dropdown counts don't add up to the total.

Each dropdown counts AWBs under the other filters, ignoring its own.

How the Breach Explorer tabs work

The six Breach Explorer tabs show how couriers keep their delivery promises. They're the SLA Breach Explorer, inside the portal. These points apply to all of them.

  • Data: built from the courier tracking data (ClickPost); Complaints also uses CX tickets, and Complaints and RTO also use ERP delivery notes. Each tab's data is built once a day at the 09:00 refresh (one tab after another, 5–10 minutes in all). Filters then work instantly in your browser.
  • Freshness: Data as of … at the top right, in your computer's time. During a refresh it says Refreshing… or Refresh queued after …, and the tab updates by itself when it's done.
  • Refresh data re-builds just that tab. If its data is under 10 minutes old it tells you when you can refresh again; if other tabs are refreshing, it waits its turn.
  • If a refresh fails, the tab keeps its previous data and shows a red banner. A failure of the morning refresh is retried automatically; one later in the day isn't, so press Refresh data again or wait for the next morning.
  • Pickers show "All" until you tick something, with Select all / Clear, and a search box for long lists. Reset all clears the tab's filters. Click a column heading to sort. "·" in a cell means zero or not applicable. Weight slabs are 0-3kg, 3+kg and unknown.
  • Delhivery Reverse shipments are left out of Breach analysis, Pendency, Complaints (delivered counts) and RTO.

Common questions

Why does Breach % change when I switch basis?

Only promises shorter than a day are judged differently. Delivered basis uses the delivery scan; RAD basis uses when the rider first reached the customer (or 5 minutes before the delivery scan if that time is missing). Whole-day promises are judged the same either way.

Why don't Under promise + Day 0 + Breach add up to Delivered?

Shipments with no promise date are counted under NA.

I can't find my city.

Destinations outside the top 130 by volume are grouped as "Other", and By lane shows only the top 60. Export CSV has every lane.

Yesterday's numbers changed today.

Promise dates are filled in a day or two late, and the data refreshes at 09:00, so recent days settle over a couple of days.

Common questions

What is "(blank)"?

A real empty value in the ticket data: many tickets have no category, issue or sub-issue filled in.

What is "Unmapped"?

Tickets that couldn't be matched to a courier (usually the AWB field holds free text). They're left out of the rates.

Why is the fake-attempt rate "a floor"?

Unmapped tickets are missing from the count but every delivery is in the base, so each courier's true rate is somewhat higher.

Why doesn't one day's rate line up?

Tickets count on the day they were raised, deliveries on the day they were delivered.

Why don't the warehouse receipts match the RTO counts?

Receipts are counted on the day the parcel arrived back, typically about 4 days after the RTO was marked.

Why did the RTO rate jump to 100%?

Common questions

Why isn't an old stuck DN listed?

Control Tower only covers DNs created in the last 7 days. Use Warehouse Exceptions → Packing pendency for older ones.

Why doesn't this match Warehouse Exceptions' Packing pendency?

They count differently:

  • Control Tower also counts Item Picked and Packed DNs with no AWB.
  • It only covers 7 days.
  • Its due dates come from promise-engine cutoffs (or the noon rule), where Exceptions uses a 14:00 cutoff.
Why does the DNs table have more rows than Pending DNs?

The table has a row per order, and a DN can carry several orders. The cards count DNs.

Where are the dropship facilities?

The Type filter starts on WH and DS. Add Dropship there.

Why didn't "By order date" change when I filtered?

That table always covers every facility, on purpose.

Common questions

What does "Process not in use" mean?

That warehouse has no not-found records in the last 30 days. Read it as "not reporting", not as zero problems.

Why is the CSV bigger than the table?

Not-found exports have a row per missing item line, and the on-screen lists stop at 250 or 300 rows.

Why does "Due today" include DNs due tomorrow?

Due today counts everything that isn't late yet, including orders placed after today's 14:00 cutoff.

Why are there fewer complaints than the CX reports?

Dark-store tickets, tickets whose order can't be traced, and customer-fault or refuted tickets are left out.

Why hasn't it changed since this morning?

It only refreshes at 09:00 or when someone presses Refresh from DB or Refresh data.

Uploading invoices

  • Click Upload invoices, add one or more files (up to 20 at a time), and click Upload. Choosing files again adds to the list.

Common questions

Why is SKU used 0?

SKU used comes only from the boxes used that editors enter. It's 0 until someone enters them for that warehouse and SKU, for the invoice's month or a later one. Use Enter usage.

I entered boxes used but SKU used didn't change.

Boxes count only against invoices dated by the end of the month they were entered for. If you entered them for a month before the invoice, they aren't counted against it. Enter them for the right month.

My invoice wasn't added. Why?

The upload message gives the reason. The usual ones:

  • It isn't a PDF.
  • It's a scan or photo, which has no text to read.
  • Its lines don't add up to its totals.
  • Its Ship To address doesn't match any of the seven warehouses.
  • It's already in Invoice Entry.

In every case the file itself is still saved in Drive.

What if I upload the same invoice twice?

It's recognised by the supplier's GSTIN and the invoice number, so it isn't added again or uploaded to Drive again. The message says it's already in Invoice Entry.

An invoice was cancelled and re-issued. What do I do?

Both copies will be in Invoice Entry, and Data checks lists them as invoices with the same lines. Check which one was cancelled (it usually shows "Credits Applied" and a ₹0 balance due) and Remove it in Invoice Entry.

An invoice went to the wrong warehouse.

The warehouse comes from the invoice's Ship To address. Check the address on the PDF. If it's a new or changed warehouse address, ask an admin to update how addresses are matched.

I can't see Upload invoices or Remove.

You have view-only access to BoxVault. Ask an admin for editor access on the Users page.

Glossary

Terms used across the portal.

AWB
Air waybill: the courier's tracking number for a shipment.
DN (delivery note)
The ERP document for an order leaving a warehouse. A return gets its own delivery note.
ERP
The company's order and warehouse system, where delivery notes, items and stock live.
ClickPost
The courier tracking service: shipment statuses, scans and courier remarks come from it.
Mother warehouse (MWH)
The seven main warehouses: Gurgaon (WH_Delhi_002), Bangalore, Mumbai (Bhiwandi), Pune, Kolkata, Chennai and Hyderabad. DS is a dark store.
RTO
Return to origin: a shipment that couldn't be delivered and is sent back. Marked is when it was flagged for return; RTO-Delivered (back at origin) is when it arrived back.
RPU / RVP
Reverse pickup: a return collected from the customer's address, rather than a failed delivery coming back.
MDND
Marked delivered, not delivered: a parcel a courier marked as delivered that wasn't received. RTO MDND checks returns marked as delivered back to the warehouse.
EDD / promise
The delivery date (or time) promised to the customer at order time, from the promise engine.
Promise engine
The system that works out each order's promise and the warehouse cutoff days.
Breach
Delivered (or still not delivered) after the promise.
Pendency
Work still waiting: orders not yet picked, shipped or delivered by when they should have been.
Ideal pickup / dispatch date
The day a parcel should leave the warehouse (Control Tower uses promise-engine cutoffs or the noon rule; Warehouse Exceptions uses a 14:00 cutoff).
OFD
Out for delivery: the first delivery attempt going out.
RAD
In Breach analysis: the rider reaching the customer. In RAD to OFD: the shipment reaching the destination hub.
QC / SDD / Standard
Promise types: quick commerce (under about 2 hours), same day (up to 1 day) and standard (more than 1 day).
P2D
Pickup to delivery, in days.
Line-haul connection
A shipment leaving the origin hub for the destination city.
HLD
Hyperlocal delivery partners (same-city).
POD
Proof of delivery: the document or photo attached to an RTO Process entry.
IGST / CGST / SGST
GST on an invoice: IGST between states, CGST + SGST within a state.
SKU
A product's code. In BoxVault, the box's code (for example DBSUPTDEL006).
Lakh (L) / crore (Cr)
₹1L = ₹1,00,000; ₹1Cr = ₹1,00,00,000.
